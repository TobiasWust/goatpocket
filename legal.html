2. Overview

GoatPocket is a dashboard for GoatCounter statistics that runs entirely in your browser. There is no GoatPocket server, no accounts, no cookies and no advertising, and the app itself contains no tracking. The only parties involved are:

  • Netlify, which serves the GoatPocket files (section 5).
  • GoatCounter, which counts visits to this website (goatpocket.com) without cookies (section 6).
  • Your GoatCounter instance, which your browser talks to directly once you connect it (section 4).

The “Buy me a coffee” link leads to Ko-fi (ko-fi.com); the links to help and source code lead to GitHub (github.com). Nothing from these services is loaded into this site. They only process data once you open one of these links, and their own privacy policies apply then.

3. Data on your device

GoatPocket stores the following in your browser's local storage:

  • the addresses of your GoatCounter sites and your API tokens,
  • the list of your sites, the selected time range and the language,
  • the most recently fetched statistics for at most five minutes, so the app starts faster.

So the app can start offline, a service worker stores the app files in the browser cache. This data only ever leaves your device towards your own GoatCounter instance (section 4), and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time: via “Disconnect all accounts” in the app's settings, or by removing this site's data in your browser.

This storage is technically necessary to provide the app you are using (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, Section 25(2) TDDDG).

4. Connection to your GoatCounter instance

When you connect a GoatCounter site, your browser fetches the statistics directly from the GoatCounter API, either at goatcounter.com or at the self-hosted instance whose address you entered. Your API token is sent along, and the operator of that instance receives technical data such as your IP address. This connection does not go through us; we receive neither the token nor the statistics. Processing by GoatCounter is governed by that operator's privacy policy; for goatcounter.com see goatcounter.com/privacy.

The demo sends no requests to GoatCounter; all numbers are generated in your browser.

5. Hosting and server logs (Netlify)

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. When you visit it, Netlify automatically processes the technical data needed to deliver it, such as your IP address, browser type and version, date and time of the request, the requested file and the amount of data transferred. We do not use this data for tracking or analytics.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.

Netlify acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Where data is processed outside the EU/EEA, this is safeguarded by standard contractual clauses.

6. Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To see how many people visit this website, the pages on goatpocket.com use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), a privacy-friendly analytics service. When a page loads, a small script from gc.zgo.at sends the page address, the referring page, the screen size, the browser and the language to goatpocket.goatcounter.com. GoatCounter derives the country from your IP address but does not store the IP address. It sets no cookies and does not track you across websites; visits are only grouped by a short-lived anonymous session hash. The app at app.goatpocket.com is not counted.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in knowing, in aggregate, how the website is used. GoatCounter is operated by Martin Tournoij; see goatcounter.com/privacy. You can block the script with any content blocker without affecting the app.

7. Retention

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it.
  • Cached statistics are no longer used after five minutes and are deleted on the next fetch.
  • Netlify keeps server logs only for as long as technically necessary.
  • GoatCounter keeps the aggregated visitor statistics until we delete them.

To exercise them, contact kontakt@tobiaswust.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal requirements change. The current version always applies.
