2. Overview

GoatPocket is a dashboard for GoatCounter statistics that runs entirely in your browser. There is no GoatPocket server, no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. Only these parties are involved:

  • Netlify serves GoatPocket's files (section 5).
  • Your GoatCounter instance, which your browser talks to directly once you connect it (section 4).

The “Buy me a coffee” link leads to Ko-fi (ko-fi.com); links to the help and source code lead to GitHub (github.com). Nothing from these services is loaded into this website. They only process data once you open such a link, under their own privacy policies.

3. Data on your device

GoatPocket stores the following in your browser's local storage:

  • the addresses of your GoatCounter sites and your API tokens,
  • the list of your sites and the selected time range,
  • the most recently loaded statistics, for at most five minutes, so the app starts faster.

So that the app also starts offline, a service worker stores the app's files in the browser cache. This data leaves your device only towards your own GoatCounter instance (section 4) and is not visible to us. You can delete it at any time: with “Disconnect all accounts” in the app's settings, or by clearing this website's data in your browser.

Storing this data is technically necessary to provide the app you are using (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, § 25(2) of the German TDDDG).

4. Connection to your GoatCounter instance

When you connect a GoatCounter site, your browser fetches the statistics directly from the GoatCounter API – from goatcounter.com or from the self-hosted instance whose address you entered. Your API token is sent along, and the operator of that instance receives technical data such as your IP address. This connection does not pass through us; we receive neither the token nor the statistics. Processing by GoatCounter is governed by the privacy policy of its operator; for goatcounter.com see goatcounter.com/privacy.

The demo makes no requests to GoatCounter; all numbers are generated in your browser.

5. Hosting and server logs (Netlify)

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. When you visit it, Netlify automatically processes the technical data needed to deliver it, such as IP address, browser type and version, date and time of the request, the requested file and the amount of data transferred. We do not use this data for tracking or analytics.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.

Netlify acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Where data is processed outside the EU/EEA, this is covered by standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it.
  • Cached statistics are no longer used after five minutes and are deleted on the next request.
  • Netlify keeps server logs only as long as technically necessary.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

To exercise them, contact kontakt@tobiaswust.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal requirements change. The current version always applies.
